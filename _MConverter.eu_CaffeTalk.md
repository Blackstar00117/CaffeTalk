Privacy Policy for CaffeTalk

Effective date: May 31, 2026

CaffeTalk is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly
- Account information such as your name, email address, and phone number when you sign up or log in.
- Order details including selected menu items, quantities, and order notes when you place an order.
- Chat messages and support requests when you use the app’s messaging features.

1.2 Usage and Technical Information
- App activity such as the screens you visit, features you use, and session timestamps.
- Device data such as device model, operating system version, app version, locale, and network type.
- Error reports, crash data, and performance information needed to maintain and improve the app.

1.3 Local Storage and Preferences
- App settings, cart contents, order history, and chat history may be stored locally on your device using shared preferences or local storage.

1.4 Optional Firebase Data
- If real-time messaging is enabled, chat conversations and user profile details may be stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the app and its features.
- Process orders and display order history.
- Enable messaging, customer support, and chat functionality.
- Authenticate your account and manage login sessions.
- Improve and personalize your experience.
- Respond to your questions and provide support.
- Comply with legal obligations and protect the app.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with:
- Service providers that help operate the app, such as Firebase Cloud Firestore when real-time messaging is enabled.
- Legal authorities if required by law or to protect rights, property, or safety.
- Other parties only when necessary to provide app services or with your consent.

4. Third-Party Services

CaffeTalk uses third-party services only as needed to support app functionality.

- Firebase Core and Firebase Cloud Firestore are used for optional real-time messaging and cloud data storage.
- The app does not currently integrate AdMob or advertising providers.
- The app does not currently use Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics by default.

These third-party services may collect and process information independently. Please refer to their privacy policies for more details.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as needed to provide the app’s services or to comply with legal obligations.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date. Please review this policy regularly.
