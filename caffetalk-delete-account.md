# CaffeTalk Account Deletion Request

If you want to delete your CaffeTalk account and associated data, please follow these steps.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to: **Dacalcap.r00117@gmail.com**
2. Include the following information:
   - your full name or account name
   - the email address or phone number used with CaffeTalk
   - a statement that you want your account and data deleted
3. We will respond within 7 business days to confirm your request.

## What data will be deleted

When your deletion request is processed, CaffeTalk will delete:

- your account profile information
- order history and saved cart data
- chat messages and support conversations
- locally stored account preferences on your device

## What data may be retained

Some data may be retained only as required by law or for fraud prevention, including:

- transaction receipts
- audit logs
- legal compliance records

This data is kept only for a limited time and is not used for marketing.
